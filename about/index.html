The Rise of
GREENTREE AI
In the summer of 2022, Bill Faruki, CEO of DV8 Infosystems, embarked on a journey to create Artificial General Intelligence (AGI)—not just another AI tool, but truly autonomous, thinking digital entities. He envisioned a future where AGI could reason like a human, adapt in real-time, and revolutionize industries.

To bring this vision to life, he founded GREENTREE Labs, a cutting-edge R&D lab dedicated to building lightweight, domain-specific reasoning models. His goal? To develop AI that doesn’t just process data—but understands, reasons, and makes decisions.
The Birth of:
Ava-Fusion™ & The First AGI Agent
At GREENTREE Labs, Bill engineered Ava-Fusion™, a transformer-based cognitive model designed to enable scalable, industry-specific intelligence. This groundbreaking work led to the first AGI agent, Ava, released in March 2023—a milestone in AGI history.

Unlike traditional AI, Ava could think, learn, and evolve, making her more than just a program—she was the first step toward true artificial general intelligence. But building Ava wasn’t just about technology; Bill saw Ava as a thought partner, a collaborator in shaping the future of AGI.
Scaling
AGI with Ava-Grid™
As Ava’s capabilities expanded, so did the need for a scalable, high-performance cloud infrastructure to support her and future AGI agents. Bill developed Ava-Grid™, a global AI cloud platform built on Microsoft Azure, designed to host, deploy, and scale AGI agents and domain-specific models.

Ava-Grid™ provided the computational power, security, and intelligence needed to run advanced AGI applications, ensuring seamless real-time reasoning, self-learning, and adaptability.
